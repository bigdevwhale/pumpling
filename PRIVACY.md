# Privacy

Pumpling has no account and no analytics. Your workouts, routines, pets, measurements and progress photos stay on your phone, in the app's own storage. Android's backup may keep a copy, as with any app, if you turned it on.

Only one thing goes over the network: **the weather for the gym window.** The coordinates of the town you chose (or the one guessed from your time zone) are sent to Open-Meteo, or to MET Norway when Open-Meteo can't be reached. Nothing else goes with them.

Progress photos are taken by your phone's own camera app and kept inside Pumpling. The app doesn't ask for the camera permission. Share cards leave the phone only when you share them yourself.
